Privacy Policy

How this website collects and uses information.

Last updated: October 27, 2026

MPA Church (Minnesota Pentecostal Assembly) respects your privacy. This page explains what information is collected when you visit this website, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Website analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website, such as which pages are viewed, how long visits last, the general area visitors come from (for example, city or country), and the type of device and browser used. This helps us improve the website.

Google Analytics uses cookies, small files stored in your browser, to collect this information. It does not tell us your name, email address, or other details that identify you personally. The information is collected and processed by Google according to Google’s Privacy Policy. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on to stop Google Analytics from collecting information about your visits.

Contacting us

Our contact form opens your own email program with your message ready to send; the website itself does not store what you type. When you email or call us, we use your name, contact details and message only to respond to you and to care for you as part of our church community. We do not sell or rent your information.

Giving

Online donations are handled by Zeffy, and Zelle payments are handled through your bank. Your payment details go directly to those services and are never stored on this website. Please see Zeffy’s Privacy Policy and your bank’s policies for how they handle your information.

Content from other services

Some pages show content provided by other companies, which may collect information according to their own privacy policies when that content loads or when you use it:

  • YouTube videos on our Live Stream and History pages (shown in YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode)
  • Google Maps on our Locations and Contact Us pages
  • Planning Center for our events calendar
  • Zeffy for our online donation form

Links to our YouTube, Facebook and Instagram pages, and to other websites, take you to sites with their own privacy practices.

Children

This website is not designed to collect personal information from children, and we do not knowingly do so.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Questions

If you have questions about this policy or your information, please contact us at webmaster@mpa.church.
