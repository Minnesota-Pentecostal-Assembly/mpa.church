To the MPA family

MPA Church is located in the Twin Cities of Minneapolis and St. Paul, MN. We exist to pursue people at all costs, so they can know Jesus, grow in Him, and serve Him daily.

Our mission statement is "making disciples who will make disciples." Services are held in English, Malayalam and Hindi.

Our vision

Three areas we focus on

Based on the great commission Jesus gave in Matthew 28:19-20.

Worship

Service to God. Worship in spirit and truth, lived out as a continuous service to God, not only songs on Sunday.

Discipleship

Service to members. Faith in Jesus is the start of the Christian life, and we prepare God's people for works of service.

Evangelism

Service to the world. We take the great commission seriously and encourage people to be witnesses of Jesus Christ.
