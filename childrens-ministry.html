For our smallest kids

Visual teaching

Kids will be kids and always want to play, so we need to draw their attention with effective teaching methods. We use visual teaching for our smallest kids.

We use the Betty Lukkens Felt Board and other electronic tools to hold their attention during the entire class, which includes Bible stories, songs, memory verses and prayer.
