Rev. Dr. Tinku Thompson

Senior Pastor

Rev. Dr. Tinku Thompson serves as the Senior Pastor of MPA Church, which he pioneered along with a few others in 2010. He also serves as Secretary and Director of Operations for ICPF International Inc. Currently he also serves as President of SFCNA.

He grew up in India, worked there for many years, and was involved in various ministries before moving to the US in 2006. He holds a Master's degree in Computer Science and has completed his Masters in Theology from the University of Northwestern, St. Paul. In 2020, he completed a Doctor of Ministry degree from Bethel Seminary, St. Paul, with a dissertation focused on Theology and Artificial Intelligence (AI).
